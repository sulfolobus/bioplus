## Reading guide (English)

### Lung Meridian (Hand-Taiyin)

Focus: the classical “route” description (from middle jiao → lung system → armpit → inner arm → wrist pulse position → thumb), and its conceptual hint that breathing rhythms and digestive rhythms are interconnected in traditional Chinese medicine theory.

### Dao De Jing — Chapter 1

Focus: the boundary of language (“the Dao that can be spoken is not the constant Dao”), and the paired perspective of:

- “nameless” as an origin point,
- “named” as the mother of the ten thousand things.

The practice implication is learning to switch between “naming/structuring” and “holding open space” without turning either into an absolute.

### Kṣitigarbha Sutra — Section 1 (opening scene)

Focus: the opening narrative sets the theme of compassion and vows through a cosmic assembly and symbolic “light clouds / subtle sounds”.
