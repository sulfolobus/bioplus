## 💡 Buying Tips

1. **Buy for your level**: Clarify your learning goals first, then choose books at the right level
2. **Prefer classics**: Classic textbooks stand the test of time — safest choice for foundational study
3. **Combine for best results**: One main textbook + one study guide + one problem set = optimal combo
4. **Digital vs. print**: Textbooks and references are better in print for note-taking; popular science works great as e-books
5. **Used books**: University textbooks can be expensive — consider used copies or library loans
